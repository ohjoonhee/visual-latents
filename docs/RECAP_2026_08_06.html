source § 1 — The original thesis

1. The original thesis

Train a VLM (Qwen2.5-VL) to emit K visual latents h ∈ R^{K×D} from an image, such that one or more frozen anchor VLMs can consume h spliced into their vision-token positions and answer questions about the image. The objective forces h to encode visual content any anchor can decode — not just the decoder used during training.

The recurring obstacle across every phase is many-to-one collapse: the K latents become near-identical. The load-bearing diagnostic is mean_off_diag_cos (mean off-diagonal pairwise cosine over the K slots). compression_ratio is blind to this failure mode, which is why early runs looked healthier than they were.

Source: README.md, docs/DESIGN.md, docs/inherited/ROUND3_POC_DESIGN.md.

source § 5 — Mechanism — where the collapse actually lives (2026-05-20)

5. Mechanism — where the collapse actually lives (2026-05-20)

Forward-pass-only probe of Monet stage2 / stage3 / post-RL, n=200 held-out Visual_CoT, A6000, 14 min.

stagelast-layer cosutil (monet_self)util (qwen_base)
stage20.369+2.717+2.145
stage30.860+0.234−0.128
rl0.861+0.118−0.131
  1. Post-RL ≡ stage 3 at every diagnostic — cos curve ±0.005/layer, PR curve ±0.005/layer, attention buckets ±0.02/layer, K×K cosine matrix bit-for-bit. RL did not recover reader-transfer.
  2. Input-driven and depth-amplified. At L0 — the latent slot's recurrence seed from the previous token's last hidden state — stage3/rl already sit at cos 0.72 vs stage2's 0.32; depth climbs them to 0.88 by L27. Roughly 2/3 of the gap is set by the seed alone, 1/3 by computation.
  3. It is NOT attention copying. The naive hypothesis is refuted: stage2 has higher mean latent_prior attention than stage3/rl at most layers (peak 0.46 vs 0.06 at L2). The locus is the output side — stage3/rl write nearly identical residual updates at every slot regardless of attention pattern. Self-reinforcing recurrence: each slot's output becomes the next slot's seed.

Source: docs/overnight_2026_05_20/REPORT.md (879 lines), harness phase0_monet_probe/mech/.

source § 6 — Dissociation 1 — geometry ≠ function (2026-05-22, n=500)

6. Dissociation 1 — geometry ≠ function (2026-05-22, n=500)

Inference-time vicreg_project (SVD-orthogonalise the K recurrence seeds, rescale to original norms, re-inject via the _seed_override_fn hook) was applied to the collapsed checkpoints.

conditioncos before → afterqwen_base utility before → after
stage30.864 → 0.329−0.115 → −0.117

The cosine signature is causally attributable to the recurrence seeds — the projection genuinely rotates the representation (CKA(stage3, stage3+vicreg_project) = 0.518) — but transfer utility does not move at all.

CKA cross-condition: rl ↔ stage3 = 0.938 (reconfirms post-RL ≡ stage 3); stage2 ↔ stage3 = 0.383.

So what: matching stage 2's cosine signature is necessary but not sufficient. Cosine is the trace, not the cause, of utility. Every success criterion after this must pair the geometry signature with a direct reader-transfer measurement.

The VICReg A/B needed a baseline that actually collapses. It does not exist.

Git archaeology first suggested epochs: models were uploaded at commit f49f382 when sft_stage3.sh had --epochs 3; a later commit changed it to 2. All training code is byte-identical between f49f382 and HEAD (0-line diff), so code drift was ruled out. The epochs hypothesis was then tested directly and also falsified.

Mechanism, now self-consistent: stage 3's all-layer cosine alignment loss makes the student match the teacher's latent geometry. The released stage-2 teacher is healthy (0.377), so a faithful stage 3 — at epochs 2 or 3 — converges to ~0.40 and stays healthy and useful. It never drifts to 0.87.

Conclusion. The released stage-3 collapse is unreproducible from the public recipe + public checkpoints. It must come from something not captured: a different/collapsed teacher, a different init, extra training, or different objective weighting. source § 12 — The blocker — Stage-2 GRPO reward ≡ 0

12. The blocker — Stage-2 GRPO reward ≡ 0

Job 221059 trained 319 steps with reward == 0 the entire time: format_reward never fired, accuracy_reward ≈ 0 — no RL signal whatsoever.

The format_reward regex is ^<|lvr_start|>.?<|lvr_end|>\s<answer>.*?</answer>$; accuracy_reward is math_verify parse-and-compare, or <answer> string match.

Ruled out, with evidence:

hypothesistestresult
skip_special_tokens=True strips the <lvr> markerschecked token flags on checkpoint-2500special=False → not stripped
the reward functions or regex are brokenCPU fp32 diagnosis, job 221327format_reward = 1.0, accuracy 1/4 → funcs fine
the SFT model produces malformed completionssame CPU runwell-formed → model fine
bf16 (rollout dtype) degrades the format--dtype bf16 re-run at n=16only 1/16 degraded → mild, not systematic
sampling config driftcompared to HF defaultstop_p=1, top_k=50, rep_penalty=1 → matches

What remains. The GPU rollout logs format_reward = 0 for all completions at every step — systematic, not random — and mean completion length is 45 on GPU vs 22 on CPU. It is DeepSpeed/multi-GPU-specific and cannot be reproduced on CPU. Commit b81ded9 added DEBUG_COMPLETIONS=1, which runs a short full-init job with DEBUG_MODE=true + LOG_PATH so reward_funcs.accuracy_reward dumps every completion and solution to a file. This has not been run.

Along the way, six further transformers-4.54.0 drift fixes went in: GRPO forward absorbing generate's flash-attn kwargs, rope via self.model, four _get_initial_cache_position call sites, attention_mask threaded through replay/score, seed_worker bound via partial, and sdpa forced for rollout (flash-attn-2 NaNs on 4.54.0).
