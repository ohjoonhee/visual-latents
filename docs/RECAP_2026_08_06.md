## 1. The original thesis

Train a VLM (Qwen2.5-VL) to emit `K` visual latents `h ∈ R^{K×D}` from an image,
such that one or more **frozen anchor VLMs** can consume `h` spliced into their
vision-token positions and answer questions about the image. The objective forces
`h` to encode visual content any anchor can decode — not just the decoder used
during training.

The recurring obstacle across every phase is **many-to-one collapse**: the `K`
latents become near-identical. The load-bearing diagnostic is `mean_off_diag_cos`
(mean off-diagonal pairwise cosine over the `K` slots). `compression_ratio` is
**blind** to this failure mode, which is why early runs looked healthier than they
were.

Source: `README.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/inherited/ROUND3_POC_DESIGN.md`.

## 3. Phases 1 / 1.5 / 1.5b / 2 — four negatives that narrowed the cause

| phase | what was tested | mean cos | verdict |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 run 1 | Qwen2.5-VL-3B FT + LVR loss, mean-MSE λ=1.0 | 0.851 | MARGINAL 2/4 |
| 1 run 2 | paper-faithful sum-MSE λ=1.0 (~2048× stronger) | **0.987** | collapse WORSE |
| 1.5 | Monet vendored class, no 4D attention mask | 0.959 | collapse |
| 1.5b | with `attention_mask_4d` injected (3 CE paths) | 0.961 | **identical to no-mask** |
| 2 | full Monet stage-2 recipe, 5K data, eff_bsz 8 | 0.737 OOD / **0.704 in-dist** | util −5.63 |

Two exonerations came out of this:

- **The loss is not the cause.** `v_roi` target cosine = 0.465 in *both* Phase-1
  runs — the ROI targets are well-distributed, so `h`'s collapse is not a property
  of the ROI gather rule.
- **Cross-slot attention isolation is not the mechanism.** Adding the 4D mask moved
  cos 0.959 → 0.961, statistically identical.

Source: `phase1_lvr/{REPORT.md,REPORT_SUMSSE.md}`, `phase1_5_attn/`, `phase1_5b_attn/`, `phase2_indist/`.

## 4. Pivot A — the breakthrough (2026-05-08)

Direct **representation-level regularization** breaks the collapse at 3B/5K.
Code at `pivot_a/` (mean-MSE LVR + regularizer, no Monet vendored class).

| variant | recipe | mean cos | qwen_base utility | verdict |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| C1 | cosine hinge τ=0.5, λ_reg=1 | 0.725 | — | MARGINAL 2/4 — hinge too weak |
| C2 | VICReg (var=1, cov=0.04, γ=1), λ_reg=1 | 0.441 | — | PASS 3/4 |
| D1 | VICReg + **sum**-MSE LVR | 0.987 | — | MARGINAL — sum-MSE incompatible (NTP starves at 3.21) |
| D2 | VICReg λ_reg=2 | **0.341** | — | PASS 3/4 |
| E | D2 @ 2× steps (2000) | 0.389 | +0.088 | PASS 3/4 |
| F | D2 @ K=4 (1000 steps) | **0.311** | +0.132 | PASS — helpful rate 50% (2/4) |
| **G** | **F + E: K=4, 2000 steps** | **0.372** | **+0.222** | **best end-to-end config** |

**Conclusion.** VICReg variance-hinge + dimension-decorrelation is the right
mechanism; mean-MSE (not sum-MSE) is the right operating form; λ_reg dose-responds
smoothly. G's cos 0.372 matches Monet stage 2's 0.375. The remaining gap is
**utility: +0.222 vs Monet stage 2's +2.19 — roughly 10×** — attributed to model
scale and data volume (3B/5K vs 7B/125K), which is what the cluster phase was
meant to close.

Source: `docs/DAY_REPORT_2026_05_08.md`, `docs/PIVOT_A_DESIGN.md`, `pivot_a/`.

## 5. Mechanism — where the collapse actually lives (2026-05-20)

Forward-pass-only probe of Monet stage2 / stage3 / post-RL, n=200 held-out
Visual_CoT, A6000, 14 min.

| stage | last-layer cos | util (monet_self) | util (qwen_base) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| stage2 | 0.369 | +2.717 | +2.145 |
| stage3 | 0.860 | +0.234 | −0.128 |
| rl | 0.861 | +0.118 | **−0.131** |

1. **Post-RL ≡ stage 3** at every diagnostic — cos curve ±0.005/layer, PR curve
   ±0.005/layer, attention buckets ±0.02/layer, K×K cosine matrix bit-for-bit.
   RL did not recover reader-transfer.
2. **Input-driven and depth-amplified.** At L0 — the latent slot's recurrence seed
   from the previous token's last hidden state — stage3/rl already sit at cos 0.72
   vs stage2's 0.32; depth climbs them to 0.88 by L27. Roughly **2/3 of the gap is
   set by the seed alone**, 1/3 by computation.
3. **It is NOT attention copying.** The naive hypothesis is refuted: stage2 has
   *higher* mean `latent_prior` attention than stage3/rl at most layers (peak 0.46
   vs 0.06 at L2). The locus is the **output side** — stage3/rl write nearly
   identical residual updates at every slot regardless of attention pattern.
   Self-reinforcing recurrence: each slot's output becomes the next slot's seed.

Source: `docs/overnight_2026_05_20/REPORT.md` (879 lines), harness `phase0_monet_probe/mech/`.

## 6. Dissociation 1 — geometry ≠ function (2026-05-22, n=500)

Inference-time `vicreg_project` (SVD-orthogonalise the K recurrence seeds, rescale
to original norms, re-inject via the `_seed_override_fn` hook) was applied to the
collapsed checkpoints.

| condition | cos before → after | qwen_base utility before → after |
|---|---|---|
| stage3 | 0.864 → **0.329** | −0.115 → **−0.117** |
| rl | 0.866 → **0.323** | −0.117 → **−0.137** |

The cosine signature is causally attributable to the recurrence seeds — the
projection genuinely rotates the representation (CKA(stage3, stage3+vicreg_project)
= 0.518) — **but transfer utility does not move at all.**

CKA cross-condition: rl ↔ stage3 = **0.938** (reconfirms post-RL ≡ stage 3);
stage2 ↔ stage3 = 0.383.

**So what:** matching stage 2's cosine signature is **necessary but not
sufficient**. Cosine is the trace, not the cause, of utility. Every success
criterion after this must pair the geometry signature with a direct reader-transfer
measurement.

Source: `docs/overnight_2026_05_22/REPORT.md`, `phase0_monet_probe/mech2/`.

## 7. Dissociation 2 — the faithful Monet stage-3 does not collapse (2026-06-04)

The VICReg A/B needed a baseline that actually collapses. It does not exist.

Git archaeology first suggested epochs: models were uploaded at commit `f49f382`
when `sft_stage3.sh` had `--epochs 3`; a later commit changed it to 2. **All
training code is byte-identical between `f49f382` and HEAD** (0-line diff), so code
drift was ruled out. The epochs hypothesis was then tested directly and also
falsified.

| ckpt | mean off-diag cos | qwen_base util | self util |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| released stage2 (= the teacher) | 0.377 | +2.05 | +2.78 |
| our stage3, epochs=2 (step 1500) | 0.420 | +0.90 | −0.51 |
| **our stage3, epochs=3 (step 2913)** | **0.401** | **+1.02** | **+0.79** |
| released stage3 (the collapse target) | **0.871** | −0.05 | +0.20 |

**Mechanism, now self-consistent:** stage 3's all-layer cosine alignment loss makes
the student match the *teacher's* latent geometry. The released stage-2 teacher is
healthy (0.377), so a faithful stage 3 — at epochs 2 **or** 3 — converges to ~0.40
and stays healthy and useful. It never drifts to 0.87.

**Conclusion.** The released stage-3 collapse is **unreproducible from the public
recipe + public checkpoints**. It must come from something not captured: a
different/collapsed teacher, a different init, extra training, or different
objective weighting. Our faithful stage 3 is a *better* model than the released
one. Collapse remains real but **config-dependent** — our deviated stage-2 repro
hit cos 0.840 / util −5.35.

HF: `ohjoonhee/vlatents-qwen25vl7b-stage3-upstream-ep3-v1` rev `step-2913`.

## 8. Dissociation 3 — collapse costs nothing at K=8, but buys K-scaling

**Same benchmark score, opposite geometry.** V\*Bench (191 questions), K=8, via
VLMEvalKit + the Monet vLLM patch:

| ckpt | latent cos | V\* Overall | direct_attr | rel_position |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| our ep3 stage3 (healthy) | 0.401 | **0.7749 (148/191)** | 0.800 | 0.737 |
| released stage3 (collapsed) | 0.871 | **0.7749 (148/191)** | 0.791 | 0.750 |

Both score the exact same 148/191; only one question shifts between splits.

**But the K-sweep separates them.** V\* at test-time latent budget K ∈ {8,10,12,16}:

| K | ep3 (healthy, cos 0.40) | released SFT (collapsed, cos 0.87) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 8 | 77.49 | 77.49 |
| 10 | 77.49 | 78.53 |
| 12 | 75.92 | 77.49 |
| 16 | 77.49 | **80.63** |
| best | **77.49** | **80.63** |

(paper Monet-SFT best-K = 82.20.)

Our repro is **K-flat** — pinned at 148/191, no benefit from extra test-time latent
slots. The released collapsed model **scales with K** (+3.1 at K=16). At the trained
K=8 the collapse is invisible; it **correlates with K-extrapolation ability**.
Whatever produced the released collapse also gave it K-scaling; our faithful repro
has neither.

Consequence: for our checkpoint K=8 is optimal, so the paper-suite gap below is
**real, not a K-protocol artifact**.

## 9. Monet paper-suite benchmark (2026-06-05)

The Monet paper (arXiv 2511.21395, CVPR 2026) evaluates only V\*, HRBench4K,
HRBench8K, MME-RealWorld-Lite (Table 3) and VisualPuzzles (Table 4 OOD). Our run is
fixed K=8; the paper uses best-K ∈ {8,10,12,16}. Suite took ~10h.

| bench | ours ep3 K=8 | paper Monet-SFT (best-K) | paper Monet-7B RL | paper Qwen2.5-VL-7B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V\* | **77.49** | 82.20 | 83.25 | 76.44 |
| HRBench4K | 62.1 (FSP 74.8 / FCP 49.5) | 68.50 | 71.00 | 68.00 |
| HRBench8K | 59.1 (FSP 70.8 / FCP 47.5) | 66.00 | 68.00 | 63.75 |
| MME-RW-Lite | 30.0 VLMEvalKit / **41.3 manual** | 52.68 | 55.50 | 45.75 |

**Confounds that must be held before claiming a repro gap:** (1) K — ours fixed 8,
paper best-K. (2) Harness residual — released best-K scores 80.6 locally vs 82.20 in
the paper (~1.6). (3) Format/parser — ep3 emits **0% `\boxed{}`**; its verbose
latent-laced output undercounts on MME-RealWorld's strict scorer (30.0 reported vs
41.3 manual re-extract, 16.8% unparseable). V\*/HRBench use lenient MCQ extraction and
are unaffected.

**Clean result:** ep3 == released SFT *exactly* at K=8 on V\* (77.49) — faithful repro
confirmed on the one parser-clean benchmark.

**Separately, the harness itself was validated** (Session A): released RL Monet-7B @
K=10 → V\* 81.68 (paper 83.25); released SFT stage3 @ K=16 → 80.63 (paper 82.20). Both
within ~1.6 pt. An earlier 23.04% was our own config error (wrong checkpoint, unswept
K, missing spawn method, missing PYTHONPATH silently disabling the patch) — not
upstream breakage.

## 10. Second upstream — LVR (arXiv:2509.24251)

Added 2026-06-05 as a second faithful reproduction alongside Monet (Monet untouched).
Qwen2.5-VL base; vision encoder + mm-merger frozen, LLM full FT. `<lvr>` placeholder
tokens are filled with ROI visual-patch embeddings; the hidden state before each
`<lvr>` is regressed toward them. `L = L_NTP + λ·MSE`, **λ=0.1, no projection head**.
Inference is fixed-budget "Fixed Token" decoding, `lvr_steps ∈ {4,8,16}`. Two stages:
Stage-1 SFT (Visual-CoT 438K with bboxes) → Stage-2 GRPO_latent RL (ViRL39K); RL is
3B-only in the paper.

Vendored into `upstreams/lvr/` and reaching the cluster by `git pull` (no rsync).

### Anchor eval — released LVR-7B, our harness (PASSED 2026-06-05)

| bench | ours s4 / s8 / s16 | paper s4 / s8 / s16 |
|---|---|---|
| V\* | 80.63 / **81.68** / 80.63 | 81.2 / 81.7 / 80.6 |
| MMVP | 72.00 / 72.00 / 71.67 | 72.0 / 71.7 / 71.7 |

Near-exact (≤0.6 pt) — the eval harness and custom steps-decoding are faithful
*before* anything was trained. (BLINK failed on a `datasets==3.5.1` feature-type
incompat — a loader-version issue, not LVR.)

### The repo does not run as published

Ten distinct defects were diagnosed and repaired to make the *explicit* recipe
execute. The headline one: README installs `flash-attn` unversioned, which today
resolves to 2.8.3 (new C++ ABI) and won't link torch 2.6.0 (old ABI). Inferred that
the authors got **2.7.4.post1** — released the *same day* as torch 2.6.0
(2025-01-29). Pinned; verified working. Others: missing Python dev headers (fixed via
uv-managed standalone CPython), unset PYTHONPATH (`$REPO:$REPO/src`), `qwen-vl-utils`
absent from requirements, `av==14.3.0` yanked + `huggingface-hub==0.30.2` contradicting
`transformers==4.54.0`, a **file never committed upstream**
(`lvr_sft_dataset_packed_fixedToken.py`, 404), a **truncated source file**
(`s3_checkpoints_lvr.py`, bare `if __name__` with no body), hard-coded Oracle-Cloud
credentials removed, and grad_accum 8→16 to hold effective batch 64 on 4 GPUs.

Source: `docs/lvr/lvr_reproduction.md`, `upstreams/lvr/CLUSTER_BRINGUP.md`.

## 12. The blocker — Stage-2 GRPO reward ≡ 0

Job 221059 trained **319 steps with reward == 0 the entire time**: `format_reward`
never fired, `accuracy_reward` ≈ 0 — no RL signal whatsoever.

The `format_reward` regex is
`^<\|lvr_start\|>.*?<\|lvr_end\|>\s*<answer>.*?</answer>$`; `accuracy_reward` is
math_verify parse-and-compare, or `<answer>` string match.

**Ruled out, with evidence:**

| hypothesis | test | result |
|---|---|---|
| `skip_special_tokens=True` strips the `<lvr>` markers | checked token flags on checkpoint-2500 | `special=False` → **not stripped** |
| the reward functions or regex are broken | CPU fp32 diagnosis, job 221327 | `format_reward = 1.0`, accuracy 1/4 → **funcs fine** |
| the SFT model produces malformed completions | same CPU run | well-formed → **model fine** |
| bf16 (rollout dtype) degrades the format | `--dtype bf16` re-run at n=16 | only 1/16 degraded → **mild, not systematic** |
| sampling config drift | compared to HF defaults | top_p=1, top_k=50, rep_penalty=1 → **matches** |

**What remains.** The GPU rollout logs `format_reward = 0` for *all* completions at
*every* step — systematic, not random — and mean completion length is 45 on GPU vs 22
on CPU. It is DeepSpeed/multi-GPU-specific and cannot be reproduced on CPU. Commit
`b81ded9` added `DEBUG_COMPLETIONS=1`, which runs a short full-init job with
`DEBUG_MODE=true` + `LOG_PATH` so `reward_funcs.accuracy_reward` dumps every
completion and solution to a file. **This has not been run.**

Along the way, six further transformers-4.54.0 drift fixes went in: GRPO forward
absorbing generate's flash-attn kwargs, rope via `self.model`, four
`_get_initial_cache_position` call sites, `attention_mask` threaded through
replay/score, `seed_worker` bound via partial, and sdpa forced for rollout (flash-attn-2
NaNs on 4.54.0).
